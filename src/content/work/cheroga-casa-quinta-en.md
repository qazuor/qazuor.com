## What it is

Cheroga Casa Quinta is a tourist rental property in Concepción del Uruguay,
Entre Ríos, Argentina — accommodation, day outings, children's parties,
weddings, and corporate events. The site at cherogacasaquinta.com is the
property's primary booking channel and the first thing every prospective guest
sees.

## The actual problem

When the owners came to me in 2021 they had:

- A working but unbranded Facebook page.
- A phone number and a few photos shared over WhatsApp.
- Real competition from other regional rentals that had been online for years
  and were ranking for the local tourism keywords.

The booking inquiry flow was entirely manual and reactive. The property itself
was the product, but the product was invisible to the people searching for it on
Google.

## What I built

This was an end-to-end engagement, not a website drop:

- **Branding**: Logo, color palette, typography, and a brand guidelines
  document. The look is meant to feel natural and elegant — the property sits on
  a green lot and that is the visual hook.
- **Web design + WordPress build**: A custom theme on Elementor with
  gallery-driven property pages, a clear services breakdown (overnight, day
  outings, events), and prominent contact / inquiry CTAs.
- **SEO**: Local keyword targeting, structured data for local business, meta
  descriptions tuned for the search terms the target guests were actually using,
  and a Google Business Profile tied to the site.
- **Client handoff**: Training on the Elementor interface and a simplified
  content update workflow. The client is not technical, and the site has to be
  maintainable by them.
- **Ongoing maintenance**: Performance monitoring, content updates for new
  services, plugin and security updates, and ad-hoc fixes as the business
  evolves.

## Why it works

The numbers from the first year after launch:

- **70% increase in Google search inquiries** compared to the pre-launch
  baseline. The structured data + local SEO work pushed the site into the top
  results for the relevant tourism queries.
- **45% improvement in booking conversion rate.** The clear service breakdown
  and the trust signals (gallery, virtual tour, testimonials) moved visitors
  from "interested" to "inquired".
- **Four years of ongoing maintenance and counting.** The site is still the
  primary booking channel. The WordPress + Elementor stack has held up to the
  point where the client runs day-to-day content updates themselves.
